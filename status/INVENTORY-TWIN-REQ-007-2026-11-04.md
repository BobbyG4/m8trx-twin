## Headline

**The people plane is real, deterministic and proven; nothing in it responds to what is on a fixture, and the sales plane is a different engine.** On `main` a shopper's fixture choice is a uniform draw inside a zone picked by role affinity, the basket is drawn *before* the walk from a category/price distribution, and the resulting `SaleCompleted` never leaves the process: `DayDrive` sends only positions, and sales reach Connect through `SaleStream`, which draws random floor EPCs with `occurred_at = now`. So none of the six truths, no sales-per-cell, no engagement, no plan-change response and no backdated history exist on `main`. Of the ten requirements: **1 met (guards), 4 partly met (traffic, impressions/dwell, determinism, shopper identity), 5 absent (sales per cell, truths, plan-change response, per-site parameter file, backdated history).**

**Second headline — two months of twin work are not on `main`.** Five remote branches form one stacked chain (`feat/history-generator-2026-10` ⊂ `restock-moves-1012` ⊂ `recent-window-1012` ⊂ PR #18 ⊂ PR #19; verified with `git merge-base --is-ancestor`). PR #19 (open, +12.5k lines, 58 files) subsumes all of it. It carries pieces this brief touches: an offline history generator that produced the mig-259 CSVs (the SQL load the brief says not to repeat), a dated `sale_event` stream that sends historical `occurred_at` over the webhook (`connect/DatedStream.kt`), and a `DayDrive` plan-epoch mode that backdates the NATS `ts`. Its own RESULTS docs say the stored times were **never read back**, so they do not answer requirement 9's re-measure. None of it makes behavior respond to placement; its sales and people planes are still separate engines (its RESULTS §5.5 says so). The TWIN-REQ-007 build (round 1) is written against `main` and does not depend on those branches; reconciling with PR #19 is a merge decision for Bob.

## What the data already supports (useful for the build)

- `reference/data/chain/stores/dec-us-denver/planogram.json`: 2,504 lines on 92 fixtures with `fixture_code`, `sku`, `ean`, `facings`, `position_sequence` — **no level field**; the directive DTO has an optional `display_level` (`connect/model/webhook/DirectivePayloads.kt:41`). Level has to be derived by a stated convention.
- `assortment.csv` carries `handle` (style; 464 handles, 350 multi-size), `size_us`, real `color` (135 values), `category`, `price_usd`, `fixture`. Enough for size-run completeness and color story without inventing attributes.
- The entrance zone `Z-01` holds three fixtures (`PI-01`, `RR-02`, `PI-03`) with **zero plan lines**, and 26 more fixtures are empty in the baseline plan (D-06 almost entirely, all GPS cases). A shopper today browses empty fixtures exactly as often as full ones.
- `epcs.csv` maps every EPC to its fixture, so a sale by EPC attributes the fixture platform-side with no new field.
