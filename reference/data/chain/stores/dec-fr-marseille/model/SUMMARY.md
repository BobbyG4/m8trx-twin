## Where the brief's numbers and the data disagreed

- Shoe boxes at the brief's ~4 facings/m cannot hold a 9+ size run on one level at one facing per EAN (40 of 67 footwear styles): 6/m used.
- Folded apparel 5/m (brief ~4/m) for the same reason (24 apparel styles have 8-15 sizes).
- Hanging: brief's 10 per facing at 25 slots/m is 250 garments a metre of rail; 2 garments per 4 cm slot-facing used.
- Bikes: 5 facings/m (3 across x 2 deep in a floor bay) so a frame-size run fits one level; 1.5/m left the two cycling wall bays empty.
- Total floor capacity (~7532 units) is about twice the 15,005 EPCs Denver holds today; the plan is a target, Phase 3 plans the transition.
- Department space cannot follow demand across departments (rule 1 fixes 16 fixtures each); it does inside each department.
- General (663 EANs) cannot use 6 fixtures at <= 6 facings per EAN: it takes what it needs and the rest spills over from hike_camp (above).
- Feature fixtures (islands, round rack, accessories walls, impulse, checkout) repeat styles already placed in a department (block > 1).
- Runs wider than any level of their class go off the floor (listed above).
